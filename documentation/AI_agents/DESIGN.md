# Look and Feel

The application uses a modern and clean design with a focus on data presentation and user interaction. It employs a light color scheme with a dark mode option for improved readability in low-light environments.

The primary colors are shades of blue, green, and gray, providing a professional and trustworthy aesthetic. The typography is clean and readable, with a clear hierarchy of information. Spacing is used effectively to create a sense of balance and visual appeal.

The user interface incorporates subtle animations and transitions to enhance the user experience and provide feedback on interactions. The overall design is intended to be intuitive and easy to navigate, allowing users to quickly access and understand the information they need.
